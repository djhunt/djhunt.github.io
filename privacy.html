Cabin Thermostat — Privacy Policy

Cabin Thermostat is used only by its owner, to control the owner's own Nest thermostat.

  • Data accessed: thermostat readings and settings (temperature, mode, Eco status) through the Google Smart Device Management API.
  • Use: solely to decide whether to change the thermostat's heat setting and to send the owner status notifications.
  • Storage: kept only on the owner's personal computer. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • Revoking access: remove "Cabin Thermostat" at myaccount.google.com/permissions or in the Nest partner connections settings.
